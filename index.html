Why one byte

A byte is the smallest unit of meaning a machine can hold. We believe that is where good software begins: one small thing, made with care.

We don't chase the app that does everything. We make the one you reach for at the right moment: the password you forgot, the ring lost in the grass, the quiet before a match. Then we refine it, review by review, until it simply belongs in your day.

That is the contribution we want to make: to put a little more possibility into every phone, wherever it is in the world, and to listen closely to the people who use what we build. Our apps are free, supported by ads, and every message you send reaches the team that made them. One byte. Endless possibilities.
